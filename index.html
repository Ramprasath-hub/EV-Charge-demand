    About Final-Year Project

    AI-Based EV Charging Demand Prediction
    Final Year Engineering Project • Department of Computer Science & Electrical Engineering

    Project Objective

    To design, develop, and evaluate an intelligent deep-learning framework (Bidirectional LSTM & XGBoost ensemble) that accurately forecasts electric vehicle charging demand across urban charging stations, mitigates transformer overload risks, and offers dynamic off-peak smart charging incentives to EV drivers.

    AI Architecture & Methodology

    • Temporal Forecasting: Bi-Directional Long Short-Term Memory (Bi-LSTM) networks capture diurnal patterns, commute cycles, and multi-day temporal dependencies.
    • Feature Engineering: Incorporates ambient temperature, weather severity, day-of-week indicators, calendar events, dynamic electricity tariffs, and traffic congestion feeds.
    • Load Balancing Strategy: Automated scheduling module shifts non-urgent vehicle charging to renewable peak generation and grid valley periods.
